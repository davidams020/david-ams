📖 How to read this — the four layers and the draw
The four reads, per timeframe
Bias — how the candle closed (bull / bear).
Displacement — close beyond the prior candle's extreme (close-based). DW+ above prior high · DW- below prior low · no DW closed back inside (a sweep, not a displacement). untested = price hasn't traded back to the displaced level yet.
Position — where price sits in that timeframe's prior range, in thirds: PREMIUM (top / above EQ — sell context) · EQ (middle) · DISCOUNT (bottom — buy context).
Character — how the close got there: swept-then-reversed vs closed-beyond-the-extreme with conviction.
The draw — higher timeframe leads
CONTINUATION / DRAW the primary path — expansion in the bias direction, or (in a pullback) the draw back toward a re-entry POI.
REVERSAL-WATCH / INVALIDATION the trigger that flips the read. When a daily move fights a displaced same-direction HTF (e.g. a daily pullback inside a bull-displaced monthly) it's a pullback: the discount/premium it draws into is a re-entry POI, and only a loss of the monthly structure is a true reversal.
Draws are points of interest, not entries. A level being reached — even an untested DW wick — is not a trade; the entry is your lower-timeframe reaction away from the wick (Asian-range failure, 15m–4H displacement, etc.).

Structure
Sequential sweeps = the order in which levels were taken (the run from one pool to the next).

📖 Opening gaps — what NWOG / NDOG mean
The gaps
NWOG — New Week Opening Gap: between Friday 17:00 NY close and Sunday 18:00 NY Globex open. Current = this week, W-1 = last week, etc.
NDOG — New Day Opening Gap: between the prior session 17:00 NY close and the new day 18:00 NY Globex open. Current = tonight, D-1 = yesterday (skips weekends).
How to use them
Each gap has a Close (Fri/prior day), Open (Sun/new day) and EQ (50% = consequent encroachment).
Bull gap = new session opened above prior close. Bear gap = opened below. An unfilled gap is a magnet.

📖 Accumulation Range model — how to read this
The two ranges
ACC range: previous close → 02:00 NY. Its high/low is a liquidity pool; the 50% EQ splits premium/discount.
Asian range (ICT): 20:00 → midnight NY — the early accumulation reference.
Premium / Discount
Price below EQ = discount → look for longs; above EQ = premium → look for shorts.
Double Discount = discount of BOTH the PD range and the ACC range → strongest long. Double Premium → strongest short.
London killzone (02:00–05:00 NY)
Enter after a stop-hunt of the ACC high/low; be out before 05:00 (new H4 open).
That stop-hunt often becomes the day’s high or low. Targets: ACC_EQ → PD_EQ.
Order flow
Shows how the range closed. The model reads a one-sided close as the opposite side building (e.g. a bullish close can mean sellside is being engineered).
MN_Open (True Day Open) is the key intraday pivot.
