📖 Opening gaps — what NWOG / NDOG mean
The gaps
NWOG — New Week Opening Gap: between Friday 17:00 NY close and Sunday 18:00 NY Globex open. Current = this week, W-1 = last week, etc.
NDOG — New Day Opening Gap: between the prior session 17:00 NY close and the new day 18:00 NY Globex open. D-1 = yesterday.
How to use them
Each gap has a Close (Fri), Open (Sun) and EQ (50% = consequent encroachment) — all act as support/resistance and draws on liquidity.
Bull gap = Sunday opened above Friday close (bullish). Bear gap = Sunday opened below Friday close (bearish). An unfilled gap is a magnet.

📖 FX4 model — how to read this
The two ranges
ACC range (FX4): previous close → 02:00 NY. Its high/low is a liquidity pool; the 50% EQ splits premium/discount.
Asian range (ICT): 20:00 → midnight NY — the early accumulation reference.
Premium / Discount
Price below EQ = discount → look for longs; above EQ = premium → look for shorts.
Double Discount = discount of BOTH the PD range and the ACC range → strongest long. Double Premium → strongest short.
London killzone (02:00–05:00 NY)
Enter after a stop-hunt of the ACC high/low; be out before 05:00 (new H4 open).
That stop-hunt often becomes the day’s high or low. Targets: ACC_EQ → PD_EQ.
Order flow
Shows how the range closed. FX4 reads a one-sided close as the opposite side building (e.g. a bullish close can mean sellside is being engineered).
MN_Open (True Day Open) is the key intraday pivot.
